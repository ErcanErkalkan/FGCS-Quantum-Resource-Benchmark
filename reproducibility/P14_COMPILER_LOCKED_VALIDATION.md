## Interpretation boundary
The compiler layer is an **independent executable realization** of weighted-cut accumulation, threshold comparison, phase marking, and uncomputation. It is not asserted to be ancilla-, arithmetic-, or synthesis-identical to the manuscript's analytical ripple-style bookkeeping template. Therefore, no exact count agreement between the two layers is claimed. The compiler evidence supports only the qualitative systems conclusion that the threshold-oracle realization can be far deeper and more two-qubit-gate intensive than low-depth QAOA under the declared compiler settings.

No physical execution, calibration, device fidelity, queue time, duration, energy, fault-tolerant overhead, quantum advantage, or hardware speedup is inferred from these results.
